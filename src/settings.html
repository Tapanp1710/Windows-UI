Settings

Appearance

Dock

Off means it behaves like an auto-hidden Windows taskbar: touch the bottom edge of the screen to bring it up.

Top bar

On, the bar slides away and comes back when you touch the top edge of the screen, and your windows get the whole screen. Off, it stays put and maximised windows stop below it, the way they stop above the Windows taskbar.

Desktop widgets

Press Enter to look the city up. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, which needs no account and no key.

Widgets sit on the desktop, behind every window, and you can drag them anywhere. Clicks between them go straight through to the desktop.

Motion

Wallpaper

Original artwork included with this project. Nothing is applied unless you choose it here, and your current wallpaper is remembered so you can go back.

Startup

Adds a shortcut to your own Startup folder. No registry keys, no admin rights, and you can remove it here or from Task Manager.

Leaving

Returning to normal Windows brings back the Windows taskbar, the work area and your wallpaper, then quits. It runs the same code as restore-windows.ps1.
